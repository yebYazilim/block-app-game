Privacy Policy

Block App Game
Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block App Game ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

We designed Block App Game to collect as little information as reasonably necessary. We do not require users to create an account and we do not directly request information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

1. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Block App Game does not provide a user registration or account system. We do not directly ask you to provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your postal address
  • Precise GPS location

2. Google Analytics for Firebase

Block App Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, to understand how the App is used and to improve gameplay, stability, and the overall user experience.

The App may record gameplay and usage events such as:

  • Opening the App
  • Viewing the main menu
  • Selecting a game mode
  • Starting or finishing a game
  • Placing the first game piece
  • Game score and gameplay statistics
  • Lines cleared
  • Lives used
  • Number of moves
  • Game duration
  • Daily goal completion
  • Reward-related interactions
  • Restart actions

These custom gameplay events are not intentionally configured by us to contain your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

Google Analytics for Firebase may automatically process technical and usage information associated with an app installation, such as app instance or installation identifiers, device information, operating system information, app version, language or locale, and approximate geographic information derived from network information, in accordance with Google's policies and documentation.

Where required by applicable law, Analytics collection is controlled based on the user's privacy choices.

3. Google AdMob

Block App Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and app information, IP address, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, advertisement interactions, and other identifiers or technical information for purposes including delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and abuse, and, where permitted by your choices and applicable law, personalizing advertising.

We do not directly access or use your advertising identifier in our own application code. Such processing may be performed by Google and participating advertising technology providers through their SDKs.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

For users in regions where consent is required, including where applicable the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Block App Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be presented with options concerning advertising and data processing.

You can revisit available privacy choices from the Privacy Preferences option in the App's settings when that option is required and available.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Block App Game stores certain game data locally on your device so that your game progress and preferences can be maintained. This may include:

  • Experience points (XP)
  • High scores
  • Gameplay streaks
  • Daily goal progress
  • Sound preferences

This information is stored locally using the App's local storage mechanisms and is not intentionally transmitted by us to our servers.

6. Firebase Services We Do Not Use

At the time this Privacy Policy was last updated, Block App Game does not use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Realtime Database, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Remote Config, or Firebase Performance Monitoring.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services provided by Google. Their processing of information is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly ask users to provide personally identifying information through Block App Game.

If the App is offered to or used by children in a jurisdiction where special rules apply, advertising and data processing practices may be subject to additional legal and platform requirements. Parents or guardians who believe that information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately may contact us using the information below.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures within our control to protect information associated with the App. Information processed by Google services is subject to Google's security practices and applicable service terms.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Data Processing

Google services used by the App may process information on servers located outside your country of residence. Such processing is handled by the relevant service provider in accordance with its applicable privacy terms and legal safeguards.

11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may provide you with rights regarding personal data, such as rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. The exact rights available to you depend on applicable law and the nature of the processing.

Where consent-based controls are available in Block App Game, you can access them through the App's privacy settings. You may also contact us regarding privacy-related questions or requests.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, the services we use, or applicable requirements change.
